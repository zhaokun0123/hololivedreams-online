Basic information

game type

Rhythm game & role-playing (Rhythm & RPG), integrating the three core gameplays of music, development and construction.

Characters

More than 50 hololive members have appeared, covering all branches such as various generations, EN, and ID.

Included tracks

It initially contains 150+ songs, including hololive original songs and classic covers, and is continuously updated.

Game Features Highlights
GAME FEATURES

Game Features Highlights

Rhythm game

The 6-key rhythm gameplay is based on the official MV and performance scenes, and supports PERFECT/GREAT/GOOD/BAD/MISS multi-level judgment and COMBO combo system.

Theme park construction

Build a dream theme park with hololive members on an uninhabited island. Build facilities, interact with characters, and advance tasks to create a unique park.

Custom music chart function

Players can create original beats and share them publicly, and play difficult, creative, and funny beats created by players around the world, with unlimited content.

Character development and formation

Develop 50+ hololive members and form a team of artists to hit high scores. There are rich strategies for team leader skills, member skills, and color attribute matching.

Collection of mini games

It includes a variety of single/multiplayer mini-games, supporting cooperation or battle. The operation is simple and easy to use. The rewards can be used for development and park construction.

Original plot

The game has a unique original plot line, unlock the story through mission advancement, and gain an in-depth understanding of the daily life of hololive members in the dream park.
