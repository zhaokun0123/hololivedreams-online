Sugar Rush Chart Guide

4

This section provides practical tips and recommended priorities for this part of hololive Dreams.

Sugar Rush, performed by Sakura Miko and Hoshimachi Suisei, has an upbeat rhythm with several demanding chart patterns.

  • Song attribute:This is a pink/sweet-type song, so pink-attribute teams receive the best bonus.
  • Difficulty:Normal is moderate, Hard is challenging, and Expert is designed for advanced players.
  • Key challenge sections:
    • Before the chorus, alternate hands accurately through the rapid notes.
    • In the middle section, keep holding the long note while hitting the scattered notes with the other hand.
    • Memorize the direction of the dense staircase pattern near the ending.

Normal is moderate, Hard is challenging, and Expert is designed for advanced players.

Tips
Event songs have reduced stamina costs and are the most efficient way to farm event tokens.
