1

Coins, Diamonds, and Stamina

Park management uses three core resources, each with different purposes and acquisition methods.

  • Coins:The basic currency used to build and upgrade facilities. Earn it from facility income, rhythm stages, and missions.
  • Diamonds:A premium currency used for character pulls, special items, and build acceleration. Earn it from missions, achievements, and purchases.
  • Stamina (LP/AP):Rhythm stages and some events consume stamina. It restores over time and can also be recovered with items.

Coins and stamina are limited early in the game, so careful planning is important.

Notice
Stamina stops recovering at the cap. Spend it before it overflows, especially during discounted event periods.

Build High-Yield Facilities First

2

Follow Missions to Unlock New Areas

The park contains multiple areas. Complete main missions to unlock additional zones and facilities.

  1. Mission-required facilities:Build mission targets first to advance the story and unlock more content.
  2. High-income facilities:Upgrade the facilities with the highest coin output in each available area.
  3. Decorative facilities:Invest in decorations after your economy is stable; they mainly improve appearance.

Upgrade costs rise quickly. Keep productive facilities at similar levels instead of maximizing only one building.

Facility Upgrade Strategy

3

Upgrade Mission Targets Before General Improvements

Facility development is a long-term system. Follow these practical priorities.

  • Prioritize missions:Complete upgrades required by main missions first because new unlocks provide greater value.
  • Focus your investment:When resources are limited, concentrate on core income facilities.

  • Collect regularly:Facility output has a storage cap, so collect it regularly to maximize income.

Watching hololive members roam the park is one of the most relaxing parts of the game. You can interact with them in several ways.

  • Say hello:Tap a member in the park to greet them and raise affinity.
  • Watch character stories:Reach the required affinity level to unlock member-specific stories.
  • Give gifts:Use gifts earned through gameplay to raise affinity quickly.
  • Take commemorative photos:Take photos with members at special locations and build a memory collection.

Members roam the park and may perform special interactions, so check different areas regularly.

Mission Progression Route

5

Prioritize the Main Story and Complete Dailies

Missions fall into several categories with different priorities.

  • Main missions:Main missions have the highest priority because they unlock story chapters, areas, and features.
  • Daily missions:Daily missions reset each day and provide consistent rewards. Complete all of them when possible.
  • Weekly missions:Weekly missions provide larger rewards and can be completed at any time during the week.

  • Achievement missions:Achievements are long-term goals and do not need to be farmed deliberately.
Tips
Do not miss daily login rewards. Even on busy days, logging in briefly preserves the reward streak.

7

Plan the Layout and Unlock Decorations

A thoughtful layout improves the park experience. Use these design principles.

  • Create themed zones:Group facilities into entrance, attraction, and dining zones for a cleaner visual layout.
  • Connect paths:Make sure every facility is connected by paths so members and visitors can reach it.
  • Add landscaping:Use plants, flower beds, and lamps to improve the scenery.
  • Use landmark buildings:Place one landmark building in each zone to create a clear focal point.

Later progression unlocks many decorations, letting you create a park that matches your own style.
